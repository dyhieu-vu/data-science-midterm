They help free up labor so that people can exploit new, more creative and interesting opportunities. Vietnamese businesses have begun researching and applying artificial intelligence in many different fields such as finance, advertising, customer care, etc. Keeping up with the pace of the economy, from 2020, the Faculty of Information Technology, Hoa Sen University has officially deployed and opened the bachelor degree program of Applied Artificial Intelligence. Program highlights 3.5 years training curriculum. Application-oriented training, Project-based learning. Students learn subjects through implementing specific, real-life projects on popular AI platforms such as Google AI Platform, Tensorflow, Microsoft Azure, or Amazon AWS. Diverse AI application in many fields helps students easily getting a job right from the 3rd year when they start studying their major.
